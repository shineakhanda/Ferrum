# Ferrum Wiki

Ferrum is a client-side Fabric mod that makes Minecraft run better on weak hardware: integrated
graphics, older laptops and Android phones.

## Pages

- [[Installation]] – which file to download, PC and Android setup
- [[Settings]] – every option, page by page
- [[Performance Guide]] – what to change first on your device
- [[Compatibility]] – other mods, hardware, Android GL layers
- [[Troubleshooting]] – crashes, black screens, missing faces
- [[FAQ]]

## Quick start

1. Install Fabric Loader and Fabric API for your Minecraft version.
2. Put the matching Ferrum jar in `mods/`.
3. Start the game. Ferrum picks a preset for your hardware on first launch.
4. Open **Options → Video Settings** to adjust it.
